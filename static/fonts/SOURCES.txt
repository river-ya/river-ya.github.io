wenkai-*.woff2: LXGW WenKai Regular v1.530, used for Chinese handwriting.
https://github.com/lxgw/LxgwWenKai/releases/tag/v1.530
These web subsets cover Chinese characters in the shipped interface and content.
wenkai-3.woff2 supplies the additional characters used in the algorithm notes and articles.

tinos-*.woff2: Latin subsets of Tinos Regular, Italic, Bold and Bold Italic.
https://github.com/googlefonts/tinos/tree/3b4482a99b80ea5fc75f187b1be3120a3f5905b3
The site uses locally installed Times New Roman first, then Tinos.
